# Ledger: decisions and backlog

One place for what the user has decided and what's still to build. Details live in the linked docs.
Started 2026-10-13.

## Decisions (user)
- **No health sponges.** Difficulty is numbers, mix and elites up to the stress-test cap (`horde_max`, 80 for now). Hammerhead's x1.8 is the one exception: it restores his fight length past his 55% gate. ([ENDLESS_MODE.md](ENDLESS_MODE.md))
- **Two modes at Ryker's:** Campaign (first dock: waves, factions, bosses, roguelike layer) and Horde (second dock: endless mixed factions, no bosses, no modifiers).
- **Bosses:** Kingpin (benched), Tombstone, Tombstone (Chained) from cycle 2, Mister Negative, Taskmaster, Hammerhead, Silver Sable. Never alone.
- **Start line, not a box:** the line spans the whole horizon at any height, both for starting and for leaving (3 s outside ends the run).
- **Every wave spawns everywhere** (no wave-1 corner), out of the camera's view.
- **Death:** respawn, drop off the perch, fade back to the Ryker's arrival point. The player decides when to leave.
- **Infighting:** allowed (fun). Enemy-on-enemy kills don't count and vanish at once (no floating heads); Campaign waves get the spawn back.
- **Roguelike** ([ROGUELIKE.md](ROGUELIKE.md)):
  - **True zero-start:** no meta-progression; only records persist.
  - **Bittersweet curses only:** every curse has an upside and a downside.
  - **Boons only touch suit powers, suit mods, gear and stats. Never Spider-Man's skills** (stripping skills would just handicap him).
  - Baseline reset at run start: **undecided** (strip-and-restore vs simulated). Research continues.
  - **The run controls the suit power and suit mods; the player can still switch suits** (user, 2026-10-13). Ideally a run starts with no power and no mods equipped (user to check if the game allows empty slots), and the waves unlock and offer them.
  - **Lock gadgets and gadget upgrades too** during runs; they become run rewards like the rest.
  - **Rewards aren't limited to the game's perks:** custom modifiers (extra damage, speed, anything that changes gameplay) are wanted. That needs its own datamining and "wacky perks" trial session **after the core system is done**.
  - **Curate the pools:** filter out suit powers and mods that don't suit combat.
