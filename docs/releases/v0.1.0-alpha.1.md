## EndlessArena 0.1.0 alpha 1

An endless-survival mode for Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered. Take a Manhattan dock circle to Ryker's Island and fight wave after wave.

> **This is an alpha.** It works, it's been played a lot by one person, and it still has bugs (below). Back up your saves before you try it.

### Requirements

- Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered, **`Spider-Man.exe` version exactly 4.0630.0.0**. Any other build: the mod refuses to touch the game. Developed and tested on the Steam version; the mod checks the exe version, not the store, but other stores' copies are untested.
- [Overstrike](https://www.nexusmods.com/marvelsspidermanremastered/mods/4199) 1.8.1 with **Enable .script support** and **Add '-scripts' to commandline.txt** turned on.
  Overstrike's stock scripts proxy has a known issue; see the [setup notes](https://github.com/Masacaai/Spider-Man-Endless-Mode/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md#2-overstrike-and-the-scripts-proxy).

### Install

1. Download **`EndlessArena.script`** below.
2. In Overstrike, add it to your mod list and press **Install mods**.
3. Launch the game (we test through **Steam**), load a save, and go to a Manhattan dock circle. Stand on it to see your best run and goals; press the START
   prompt to travel to Ryker's, then walk into the yard to begin.

Your settings and records are kept in `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\EndlessMode\`, so reinstalling or updating Overstrike mods does not reset them.

To remove it: remove the mod in Overstrike and press Install mods again (you can also delete `scripts\EndlessMode*` from the game folder).

### What's in it

- **Campaign** (first dock): chapters of 4 waves, one faction each, a boss every 4th wave, tougher cycles forever. Pick 1 of 3 **boons** (suit powers, suit
  mods, stat boons) after each wave and a bittersweet **curse** after bosses. You start with no suit power or mods and earn them; your real loadout is saved and
  restored at the end (and on the next launch if the game crashes mid-run).
- **Horde** (second dock): one continuous riot, factions join as it goes, no bosses. A style meter with DMC-style ranks (Puny Parker up to Superior),
  combo bonus and survival bonus.
- Results panels at the end, a live score panel in Horde, boss entrances announced with the game's crime pop-up, an abandon prompt if you leave the yard, and
  controller-aware prompts (keyboard, Xbox, PlayStation).
- Run telemetry (health over time, hits taken, attackers) written to `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\EndlessMode\EndlessMode_runs\`, with a viewer in the repo (`tools/runviewer`).

### Known issues (see the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Masacaai/Spider-Man-Endless-Mode/issues))

- Occasional white cubes, floating heads or invisible enemies if the game runs out of model room; the mod caps how many enemy types it loads
  (`[run] max_types` in `Documents\Marvel's Spider-Man Remastered\EndlessMode\EndlessMode.ini`; lower it if you see this). #3
- One game freeze seen once during Horde. #4
- Heavies/miniguns often stand around without engaging. #1
- Enemies can get stuck in cages. #6 Bodies stand back up into a fight pose. #5
- The Horde results panel may be missing its buttons or show wrong tier labels once a score tier is reached (a fix is included but untested). #2
- Mister Negative sometimes doesn't engage. #7
- Some changes in this build were not yet tested in game: slow motion on death, the Campaign results panel scoring, the two-row dock panels, and the new settings/records location.
