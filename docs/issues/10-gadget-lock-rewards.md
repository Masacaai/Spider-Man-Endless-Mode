---
title: Lock gadgets and make gadgets run rewards
labels: feature, roguelike, research
---

Suit powers and mods are locked and earned during Campaign runs; gadgets (web shooters, trip mines, etc.) and gadget upgrades are not. Find the gadget equip path (the quick-select wheel,
`UIQuickSelectWheelEquip`) and the gadget upgrade items; then hook and lock them like `runloadout` does for suit slots (`lock on`), and offer gadgets as rewards.
