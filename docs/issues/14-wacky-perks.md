---
title: Custom perks beyond the game's items
labels: feature, research, roguelike
---

Rewards need not be limited to the game's perks: custom modifiers (extra damage, speed, movement, focus, gadget ammo) are wanted. This needs datamining the levers the game exposes (damage in/out, heal and lifesteal are ready;
focus meter, gadget ammo and max health need RE) and a prototype session to try "wacky" coded perks.
